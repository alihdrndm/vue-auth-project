Design system

Tokens, type, components and status labels for the Eingang app and its homepage. Token and component names match the code one-to-one. Every colour on this page is a token.

Drawing style

The mess is drawn; the product is not. Printed sheets on the desk with crop marks and a strip of tape, a red ballpoint, one highlighter. The pen draws itself when the sheets scroll into view.

Highlighter 450 ms, circle 700 ms, arrow 400 ms, note 500 ms, staggered, ease-in-out. With reduced motion, everything is drawn from the start.

Pen--pen, 2.2 px, round caps, ink filter. Circles overshoot their start; arrows end in two short strokes. Never on app UI.
NotesCaveat 600 at 24 px or more, lowercase, one short thought, tilted a few degrees.
Highlighter--hl behind ink text, skewed, ragged ends. One swipe per sheet at most.
Sheets--paper with --shadow-sheet, crop marks in --line-strong, tape in --line. At most two drawings of the mess per page.
