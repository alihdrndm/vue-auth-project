Some of our invoices are e-invoices. We couldn’t tell you which.We checked every invoice by eye. The one with the new bank account looked fine too.We paid the same invoice twice. Both copies looked perfectly valid.

Eingang puts every supplier invoice, XML or PDF, in one inbox and tells you which ones are valid e-invoices. It flags duplicates and changed bank accounts, and someone else approves before anything goes to the tax advisor.

  • Receiving: since 1 Jan 2025

    Every German business must be able to receive structured e-invoices (EN 16931).

    Source: European Commission
  • Sending: from 2027 and 2028

    Plain PDF invoices to other German businesses end: in 2027 above €800,000 turnover, in 2028 for everyone. Invoices up to €250 stay exempt.

    Source: European Commission
  • Checks the official EN 16931 and XRechnung rules

    Every verdict names the rule it rests on and says in plain English what to do.

  • AI shows where every value came from

    For plain PDFs, each value comes with the text it was read from. Scans have no text, so those are typed in by hand.

  • Open source, MIT

    The code is on GitHub. Read it, check it, change it.
